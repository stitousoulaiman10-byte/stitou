**Memory and brain health after 40 is a proven hook for this audience.** The coffee video also leans on "ذاكرتك وتركيزك", and it is your second-fastest video. That's why the ebook is built around the 60-second exercise and extends it into a full 30-day plan.

Supporting signals:
- Arabic health books keep selling around *natural* and food-as-medicine angles: الطب النبوي, صيدلية الطبيعة, الغذاء دواء. The ebook uses Arab-kitchen foods (dates, pomegranate, lentils, olive oil, sardines, mint tea) and faith-friendly habits (walking to the mosque, memorising verses, أذكار النوم). It does this without making remedy claims.

## 2. Who buys

| Buyer | Who they are | What makes them buy | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The worried 45–65 viewer** (main buyer) | Watches on phone, often in KSA/UAE/Kuwait/Egypt/Morocco. Notices forgetting names and keys. | Fear of «الزهايمر», wants a simple plan, trusts the channel | «خطة بسيطة 30 يومًا، بدون أدوية، من مطبخك» |
| **B. The adult child (28–45) buying for a parent** | Higher card and PayPal use, more comfortable buying online | Guilt plus love: a useful **gift for أبي/أمي** | «أجمل هدية لوالديك: ذاكرة أقوى» |
| **C. Students / busy professionals** | Want focus | Focus, sleep, coffee timing | Secondary. Don't target them in ads |

Buyer B is the key to conversion. Older viewers watch, but their children often pay. Put a gift line on the sales page and in the pinned comment.

Purchasing power: Gulf buyers (SA/AE/KW/QA) can pay the full price. Egypt and the Maghreb need a lower regional price (see pricing).

## 4. Where to sell (check each platform's current terms before launch)

- **Gumroad.** Fastest to set up. Takes cards and PayPal, and has a built-in purchasing-power-parity discount option. Recent 2026 guides say its fees are about 10% or higher.
- **Payhip.** Often recommended as a cheaper alternative (about 5% on the free plan). Takes PayPal and Stripe cards and can stamp PDFs against piracy.
- **Salla / Zid (Saudi stores).** Worth it once Saudi sales grow: local payment methods (mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay) convert much better there.
- Avoid platforms that block payouts to your country. Test a real purchase from a Gulf card and a Moroccan card before launch.

## 5. Funnel from the channel

1. **Free lead magnet:** export page 15 (the 60-second exercise) as a one-page PDF «هدية مجانية». Give it away for an email or a WhatsApp opt-in. A WhatsApp Channel works very well with Arab audiences 40+.
2. **Pinned comment + first line of description** on every memory, brain or sleep video:
   > 📘 حمّل كتاب «ذاكرة أقوى في 30 يومًا» — خطة كاملة + وصفات + جدول متابعة 👇 [link]
3. **Dedicated launch video:** «جرّبت خطة 30 يومًا للذاكرة… هذا ما حدث». Walk through the book on screen.
4. **End screens and community posts:** show the cover image (`cover.png`) as a poll: «ما أكثر شيء تنساه؟», then link the book.
5. **Shorts:** one tip per Short from the book (each of the 7 thieves, each of the 10 foods), ending with «الخطة كاملة في الكتاب، الرابط في التعليق».

## 8. Compliance (important for an AI-avatar health channel)

- Don't present the AI presenter as a real, licensed doctor. The book is signed «فريق صحة نوفا» on purpose.
- Keep the medical disclaimer (page 2). Don't promise cures; the book is written with "linked to" and "may" language.
- YouTube requires creators to disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content. Use the "altered or synthetic content" setting when you upload.
- If you add your avatar image to the cover (see README), use an image you own the rights to.
